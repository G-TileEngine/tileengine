## Where QA has been too relaxed

Plain words, from the same reviews:

- **Carries were pushed step to step.** Step 18’s empty-id / missing-`effects` ruling was parked for step 19; step 19 r1 still threw and could unlock `""` after a pool edit; only r2 closed the carry. The r2 leftovers (sparse `effects`, getter / Proxy vs `packAction`, the general `submit` guard, `chaos-gen`’s live mix) were again taken as “fair deferrals” or “not a send-block.”
- **“Typography is not a reject” hid real grep weakness.** SE-01 R5b / R8b failed on proto line-breaks and `error:` literals (C4). Several earlier steps logged number-literal misses (`10_000`, `.5`) as optional tightens and left them open in the invariants doc.
- **“Residual, not a hold” became a shelf.** No general string cap, `stored` keeping over-cap parts, and a package-narrow depcruise hole were all accepted on SE-01. `(e)6` / `(e)8` stayed report-only after a known throw (B2).
- **Device and export work is still on the shelf since 2026-11-09.** Baseline export audit BLOCKED; mobile was a static scan. The painter plan’s phone self-test and `te-classic@1` matrix have not been closed in these QA docs. A static import scan was counted as an “export audit” on SE-01.
- **Smoke pins arrived late.** Step 15 printed exploit smoke lines and did not assert them (a silent bucket change stayed green). Step 16 did close that residual with `assert.equal`. Do not repeat the printed-only pattern.
