## A. One ability's effect timeline (the SkillRig "order")
1. The order is the JSON, not the sentence. If the paragraph and the JSON disagree, the JSON wins (`normalize` is what plays). — `SKILLRIG.md:161`; implement the JSON, not a guess from the paragraph — `SKILLRIG.md:173`.
2. Loading a block reads from the first `{` to the last `}`, then normalizes: a bad block is ignored; a bad colour falls back to that field's default; board size, counts and seconds are clamped. — `SKILLRIG.md:163`. (TileEngine: each clamp is a notice, warn don't block — `core-spine-slices.md` X14.)
3. Spawn is chosen once for the whole ability, before any step: drop on the aim, from the caster, beside the caster, or in front of the caster. — `SKILLRIG.md:77-84`; apply `spawn`, `aim`, `drop` — `SKILLRIG.md:177`.
4. Steps (`frames`) play **in list order** on the game's clock; each lasts its `seconds` (0.20–3.00). Six steps at most. — `SKILLRIG.md:176`, `:86`, `:36`.
5. Earlier steps are the wind-up; **the hit connects on the last step**. — `SKILLRIG.md:126`.
6. The hit applies at `connect` through the last step, not when that step ends: time = start of the last step + `connect` × that step's `seconds`. `0.92` is just before the end; an old reel without the field connects at `0.42`. — `SKILLRIG.md:178`, `:165`.
7. `damage: null` means no damage: do not invent a number. — `SKILLRIG.md:178`, `:159`, `:27`.
8. `impact` (burst) is drawn only when present, `trail` only when true. — `SKILLRIG.md:179`, `:139-140`.
9. Per step, look channels (tint, scale, glow, fade) ramp across that step with `look.ease`; a null channel holds `blade`; the trail stays the saved blade. — `SKILLRIG.md:176`, `:110`.
10. A second motion inside a step starts late by a fraction of the step and finishes when the step finishes; it adds no step and does **not** move the hit. — `SKILLRIG.md:112`.
11. Release (step 2 on; straight, swerve, bounce only): all at once, or split and curve, leaving together in time. — `SKILLRIG.md:101-106`.
12. Copies (row, spin, free-fire) may differ by jitter, start spread and size, yet **arrive together**; the same numbers every play. — `SKILLRIG.md:116`, `:176`.
13. Bounce hits the tapped squares **in tap order** (≤ 8). — `SKILLRIG.md:94`.
14. Free fire passes through the aim and continues `pierce` tiles; it does not stop on the first body. — `SKILLRIG.md:91`.
15. Hook and reel: **Yank** pulls the one enemy all the way to the landing tile (empty landing → the tile in front of the tie). **Chain** hits the enemy, then each extra tapped body **in order**; each body moves one tile closer to the one before it. — `SKILLRIG.md:98`.
16. A reel still pulls when the hook connects even if the hit is None; Knock 1 on a reel is extra. — `SKILLRIG.md:142`.
17. Hit kinds: None; Nudge (shove then settle back on the same square); Knock 1 (one tile away from the caster; the board edge stops them); Split (no nudge, no knock). — `SKILLRIG.md:134-137`.
18. Loop and speed are preview only; 1× is the game. — `SKILLRIG.md:38`. (TileEngine: game speed is the clock's `timeScale`; step size never changes — ARCH §2.3.)

## B. Targeting and arrival (the two pipes)
19. UNIT (every basic): aims at the living body; follows it if it moved; cannot be dodged; a dead target is never fired at its last cell — pick another legal living target or idle. — `COMBAT_TARGET.md:9-12`.
20. TILE (every damaging skill/ult/skillshot): pick the cell first; play the animation either way; **on arrival** a living occupant takes the damage, an empty cell is no damage; never retarget mid-flight. — `COMBAT_TARGET.md:16-20`.
21. Basics read the drawn cell every shot; skills read the cell chosen at cast. — `COMBAT_TARGET.md:75`.
22. Stored id + cell (Gale needles): on connect the living id wins; if gone, whoever stands on the stored cell is hit; empty cell misses. — `COMBAT_TARGET.md:49`, `:72`.
23. On 0 HP: `alive = false`, removed from target lists **that frame**; nobody aims at a dead id or at a cell that only used to hold it. — `COMBAT_TARGET.md:31-32`.
24. Multi-hop strikes skip hops with no living target rather than firing into empty tiles. — `COMBAT_TARGET.md:36-37`.
25. A card is **aim, then cast, then apply**. — `COMBAT_TARGET.md:57`. A wrong click leaves the card in hand. — `CardsGuide.md:5`.
26. A unit leaving a cell empties it that frame; no occupancy stays on a lunge cell. — `COMBAT_TARGET.md:63-64`.
27. Status effects (poison tick, shields) are on the unit id, not a pipe. — `COMBAT_TARGET.md:73`.

## C. Effects on the board (sequence words the docs state)
28. A spell shot travels from caster to target; **the turn waits until it lands**. — `SPELL.md:98`.
29. Adjacent splash: each extra ball leaves the first target **only after the previous ball has hit and faded**; one neighbour at a time, **N, E, S, W**; same damage; then the arm is spent. — `SPELL.md:106`.
30. A column Special walks **north to south**; the tide walks a row **right to left**. — `SPECIAL.md:35`.
31. Cataclysm: seven random tiles erupt **one after another**. — `TERRAIN.md:56`.
32. Visual timing sets hit time: Earthen Spike damage lands when the tips meet (`TERRAIN.md:64`); Divine Retribution's hit lands when the fall finishes (`SPELL.md:287`); Detonation debris deals damage when it hits (`TERRAIN.md:72`).
33. Heavenly Sword: 2 damage and knock back one tile away from the warrior; if that tile is off the board or occupied they stay **and still take the hit**. — `SPELL.md:176`. Knockback Shot: blocked tile or edge keeps them in place; they still take the hit. — `SPELL.md:148`.
34. Knock onto a trap: the sword knocks the target onto the trap, **then** the trap deals 1 and stuns. — `STRATEGY.md:21`. A trap fires when a piece steps on it or an enemy spawns on it: 1 damage, stun 2 turns, then it disappears. — `SPELL.md:158`.
35. Get Over Here: the chain locks the **first body** it reaches and pulls it to the open tile in front of the warrior; a body in the way stops it on the last open tile before it. — `SPELL.md:186`.
36. Shield: hits land **on the shield first**; when it breaks it is gone. — `SPELL.md:166`.
37. Guards: Protective Melody ignores the next spell hit (a 0 pops, then the guard is gone); Resistant Melody ignores the next basic. — `SPELL.md:208`, `:212`.
38. Melody buffs are on the target **before** the note leaves. — `SPELL.md:192`. Soothing removes one debuff each, **then** draws 1. — `SPELL.md:196`.
39. Cost before effect: "the cost is paid first" (Solidarity, Lean on Me). — `SPELL.md:273`, `:285`.
40. Back to Back: mana per body on the line, the plate clears when the shot is done, **then** draw 1. — `SPELL.md:142`.
41. Poison Arrow: the basic hits for normal damage, **then** the victim is poisoned (3 of their turns, 1 at the start of each). — `SPELL.md:132`.
42. Victory in Death: on death in its window, fireballs circle the body for 1.3 s, **then** each hits a random enemy. — `SPELL.md:66`.
43. Last Stand: the unit cannot die until your next turn starts; poison ticks **wait** and come back when the stand ends; a Sacrifice death is **held** until the stand ends. — `SPELL.md:64`, `:76`.
44. Divine Retribution: if the hit kills, the card returns to hand; it costs 1 more each replay; a returned card is discarded at turn end. — `SPELL.md:287`.
45. Stronger Together's extra leaves after that hit; Empowering's extra stays until that attack is spent. — `SPELL.md:269`, `:204`.

## D. Turn boundaries
46. A side has two actions; a move is one, an attack is one; spells spend mana, not actions; when both actions are spent the turn passes. — `HOW_TO_PLAY.md:42`, `SPELL.md:7`.
47. Start of turn (stated items, **no relative order given**): mana +1 after the first turn (`SPELL.md:9`); draw 1 if the hand is ≤ 4 (`SPELL.md:3`); poison ticks (`SPELL.md:132`); summoned acolytes step or bite (`SPELL.md:232`); starter acolytes step (`SPELL.md:236`); Last Stand ends (`SPELL.md:64`); avatar acts (`HOW_TO_PLAY.md:72`).
48. The turn fuse does not tick during a spell's flight or its pause. — `ASCENSION_TOWER.md:59`. (BTF docs say 30 s, `ASCENSION_TOWER.md:59`, `HOW_TO_PLAY.md:3`; TileEngine's BTF preset default is 60000 ms, user-adjustable, CEO 2026-11-09.)
49. Lane rotation order (Bloodline lane): `skill: "in"` on arrival; `"after"` waits for the hit count; `ult` after hits and after skill-in/after; `midHits` after that skill, before the ult; `"postUlt"` after the ult clip. — `GAMEPLAY_SYSTEM.md:72-76`.

## E. TileEngine rulings already in force (engine side)
50. `landedOn` fires on every move end, never mid-path; `pushBlocked` fires before `landedOn` when both happen; edge walls always stop forced movement. — `handoff/steps/step-04-units-occupancy.md:19-21`.
51. `kills_on_landing` (rules side) resolves on `landedOn`, never on pass-overs. — `data/core-types.ts:115`, `:129`.

53. All effect timing is integer sim ms on the one clock (CN-001); FX play on the render phase of the same clock. — ARCH §2.3, §12.10.

## Silent spots: answers (2026-11-09)
Every spot is a **project option**; the value below is the **PROPOSED BTF-preset default** taken from what BTF's code does unless marked otherwise. Spots 1, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13 and 15 are **DECIDED**; spot 10 is a **CoS default (no live case)**. "Booth" = the SkillRig preview (`skillrig/paint.ts`, `skillrig/spec.ts`); "game" = tower chess (`game/*.ts`). Where they disagree, that is stated.

1. **Damage vs knock in one hit.** — **DECIDED by the principle (CoS 2026-11-09): current play.** Code: damage first (`soak`), then the knock **only if the target survived**. Sword: `game/towerChessSpells.ts:1413-1414`; Knockback Shot on a basic: `game/towerChess.ts:1787` then `:1799` (`hit.hp > 0 && takeKnock`). A killing hit does not knock, so no landing effects. Option: `hit_knock_order: damage_then_knock_if_alive` (alt. `knock_then_damage`, `damage_then_knock_always`).
2. **Trap and forced movement.** Code: every placement trips a trap, whatever moved the body: `place()` calls `tripTrap()` (`game/towerChess.ts:536-540`), and knocks/shoves use `place()` (`game/towerChessSpells.ts:1267`, `game/towerChess.ts:1764`). Because of spot 1, a body killed by the hit is never knocked onto the trap. Trap: 1 damage via `soak`, then stun +2 (`game/towerPierce.ts:725-734`). Option: `trap_trigger: any_move_end` (alt. `voluntary_steps_only`).
3. **Damage layers.** Code (`game/towerStrike.ts:482-517`, `soak`), in order: talent veil (`:494`) → Protective guard for spell/arrow hits (`:499`) or Resistant guard for basics (`:503`) → Lucifer shield (`:507`) → Fortification shield absorbs the rest (`absorbWard`, `:511`; `game/towerBuffs.ts:220-226`) → HP loss (`:515`) → Last Stand (`immortal`) clamps HP to 1 (`:517`). Each guard is consumed only if damage is still > 0. Option: `damage_layers` (ordered list; preset default as above).
4. **Damage modifiers.** Code: all additive and summed **before** any guard/shield: `pieceStrike` = base + power (Empowering/Stronger Together) + body attack (`game/towerStrike.ts:223-232`), plus Fire Storm +1 (`game/towerChess.ts:1779`), then passed to `soak`. Contest absorb applies after the sum (`:1785`). Option: `modifier_stacking: additive_before_mitigation`.
5. **Start-of-turn order.** — **DECIDED (CoS 2026-11-09; principle: current play).** Code (player side, `openYou`, `game/towerChess.ts:1119-1167`): discard the rival's returned Retribution cards (`:1120`) → summoned acolytes act (`:1124`) → tide (`:1127`) → actions reset (`:1128`) → Last Stand ends (`:1140`) → garden (`:1142`) → held Sacrifice deaths (`:1143`) → taunt clear (`:1144`) → Victory-in-Death window ticks (`:1145`) → avatars act (`:1147-1151`) → fuse refill (`:1158`) → mana +1 (`:1162`) → ring collapse (`:1163`) → Enhanced count (`:1164`) → stun (`:1165`) → poison (`:1166`) → draw (`:1167`). The rival side (`openThem`, `:1040-1096`) differs: it draws **before** stun and poison (`:1081-1083`) and runs summoned acolytes earlier (`:1070`). **Ruling:** one order for both sides, the player-side (`openYou`) order above. Option: `turn_start_order: player_side` (default; ordered list) | `rival_side` (the `openThem` order).
6. **End-of-turn order.** Code: there is no end-of-turn phase. End-of-turn work runs at the top of the next side's start: the ending side's returned Retribution cards are discarded first (`game/towerChess.ts:1042`, `:1120`). The victory check is not turn-tied; it runs every tick (`game/towerChess.ts:764`). Option: `turn_end_effects: at_next_side_start` (TileEngine has a real `sideEnd` phase, STATES §3, so the preset maps these there).
7. **Death sequence.** — **DECIDED (CoS 2026-11-09; principle: current play).** Code inside `soak` (`game/towerStrike.ts:517-527`): Last Stand clamp → Victory in Death fires (`:518`) → opening-acolyte death hook (`:519-521`) → slave kill XP (`:522`) → starter-acolyte kill reward: mana +2, draw, killer +1/+1 (`:523`, `:464-475`) → defeat note (`:524`) → ranked kill note (`:525-529`). Then the caller: basic attack → taunt end/refund (`noteFutileHit`), poison, venom, Back-to-Back mana, knock-if-alive, special meter (`noteProfile "kill"`) (`game/towerChess.ts:1795-1801`); sword → Retribution `onKill` (card return) before the special meter (`game/towerChessSpells.ts:1419-1422`). Dead bodies leave target lists via `hp > 0` filters (`living()`). The callers place the special meter differently relative to the other kill effects. **Ruling:** one order for every caller: the `soak` chain, then the kill rewards and other kill effects (incl. card return), then the **special meter last**. Option: `death_order: reward_then_meter` (default; ordered list) | `basic_attack_path` | `sword_path` (the two caller orders as recorded above).
8. **Multi-body hits.** Code: Piercing Shot tiles are ordered **near to far** from the caster (`pierceTiles`, `game/towerPierce.ts:361-370`), and each body is hit when the shot reaches its tile (`:115-131`). Per-body effects (poison, mana) are applied per hit as the shot passes. Option: `multi_hit_order: near_to_far`.
9. **Pierce hit timing.** — **DECIDED (CoS 2026-11-09; principle: live play beats the preview).** Live game: per tile, as the projectile passes (`game/towerPierce.ts:116-118`, `arrowLands` `:41-49`). SkillRig preview (booth): one hit at `connect` on the single foe (`skillrig/paint.ts:427-438`). Default = the live per-tile hit. Option: `pierce_hit_timing: per_tile_on_pass` (default) | `all_at_connect` (the preview).
10. **Chain reel.** — **CoS default (no live case), 2026-11-09.** The booth models only the first body: it moves one tile toward the anchor at connect (`skillrig/paint.ts:445`, `oneToward` `skillrig/spec.ts:574-579`), and extra links are only path points (`skillrig/spec.ts:594`). A pull onto an off-board tile stays put (`:578`); occupancy is not checked in the booth. No game implementation of multi-body chain was found. **CoS default:** per body, **near to far**: hit, then that body's one-tile pull toward the anchor; if the destination tile is blocked (wall, closed tile, body or off-board), there is **no pull** and the body stays. Option: `chain_order: hit_then_pull_per_body` (default, near to far).
11. **Reel + Knock 1.** — **DECIDED (CoS 2026-11-09; principle: current play).** The code contradicts the doc. With a reel as the last step, the booth applies only the pull; the Knock 1 branch is an `else if` and never runs (`skillrig/paint.ts:440-455`), while `SKILLRIG.md:142` says Knock 1 "is extra". Get Over Here in the game deals no damage and has no knock. Default = pull only (current play). Option: `reel_knock: pull_only` (default) | `pull_then_knock1` (`SKILLRIG.md:142`; flagged **`doc_differs`**: the doc's reading, not current play).
12. **Bounce/edge re-hits.** Code: the booth hits once at `connect`; edge stop/clip/bounce only changes the drawn pose (`skillrig/paint.ts:209-215`, `:427-438`). In the game a body is hit at most once per tile index (`hitIds` key `id:n`, `game/towerPierce.ts:128-129`). Option: `rehit_same_body: never`.
13. **Landing kills vs hit damage.** — **DECIDED by the principle (CoS 2026-11-09): current play.** Code: hit damage first, then (only if alive) the knock calls `place()`, which trips a trap **before** the landing kill on a collapsed tile (`game/towerChess.ts:536-545`: `tripTrap` `:540`, then `tileToppled` `:541-545`). Order: hit → knock → trap → landing kill. Option: `landing_order: hit_knock_trap_landing`.
14. **Simultaneous effects.** Code: "at the same time" is two sequential swings, anchor first, mate second (`game/towerChoirCards.ts:900-903`); each swing pays on its own (`paySwing`, `game/towerChess.ts:1768-1775`), and if the first kills, the second finds no living target. Multi-target cards loop in list order. Option: `simultaneous_hits: sequential_list_order`.
15. **Both sides wiped at once.** — **DECIDED (CEO 2026-11-09 "draws are allowed", relayed by CoS):** the result is a **draw**. Project option **`simultaneous_wipe: draw | attacker_wins | defender_wins`**, default **`draw`**. Note: BTF's `judgeBoard` returns without a result when both sides are at 0 before time-up (`game/towerChess.ts:1749`); TileEngine does not copy that.
16. **Random-pick order.** Code: unseeded `Math.random` in call order, e.g. shuffles and picks in `game/towerTerrain.ts:221`, `:669`, `game/towerChoirCards.ts:1008`, `game/towerChess.ts:1106`. Not replayable. TileEngine: seeded `core/rng` streams, drawn in the order the effect lists its picks. Option: `rng_policy` (existing seed policy, ARCH §9).
17. **Stun counting.** Code: counted at the **start of the victim's side turn**: if `stun > 0`, the piece is locked for that turn and the count drops by 1 (`tickStun`, `game/towerChess.ts:334-342`; called at `:1165`, `:1082`). A stun applied during the victim's own turn does not lock until their next turn start (`pieceStunned` reads the lock, `:320-322`). Option: `stun_count: victim_turn_start`.
18. **Last Stand release.** Code: Last Stand ends at the owner's turn start (`expireImmortal`, `game/towerChess.ts:1140`), before held Sacrifice deaths (`:1143`) and before poison (`:1166`). Poison does not tick while untouchable, and its count does not drop (`game/towerBuffs.ts:207-217`). So the waiting ticks resume that same start of turn, after the held death resolves. Option: `last_stand_release: before_held_deaths_and_ticks`.
19. **Fuse and in-flight effects.** Code: the fuse runs only when no card aim is open, no contest is resolving, no spell is busy and no warp aim is open (`game/towerChessClock.ts:121`). "Spell busy" covers held fire, sword drops/volleys, splash and unresolved shots (`game/towerChessSpells.ts:178-186`), but **not** basic bites or knocks. Expiry waits until nothing is busy, so it never fires mid-flight (`:128`). Option: `fuse_pause_during: spells` (alt. `all_resolution`).
20. **Effects crossing in flight.** Code: a moving unit is re-placed immediately (`place`, `game/towerChess.ts:536`); an in-flight TILE shot checks the living occupant only when it reaches the tile (`game/towerPierce.ts:125-127`). So whichever happens first in time wins; same-frame order follows the update order in `tickTowerChess` (**ambiguous**, frame-dependent). TileEngine: order by sim-ms due time, ties by event `seq` (one clock). Option: `crossing_order: by_due_time_then_seq`.
